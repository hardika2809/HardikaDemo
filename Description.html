Eco-Friendly Stainless Steel Water Bottle

Our eco-friendly stainless steel water bottle is designed to keep
         you hydrated while reducing single-use plastic waste. Built with
          durability and style in mind, it’s the perfect companion for your
           daily routine, workouts, travel, and outdoor adventures.

Key Features

  • Premium BPA-free stainless steel
  • Keeps drinks cold for 24 hours, hot for 12
  • Leak-proof screw-on lid
  • Lightweight and portable
  • Available in multiple colors

Specifications

Capacity: 750 ml (25 oz)

Material: Stainless steel Dimensions: 10” × 3”

Weight: 350 g

Colors: Black, Silver, Blue, Rose Gold
